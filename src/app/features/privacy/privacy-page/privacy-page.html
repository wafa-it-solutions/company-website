Privacy

Your privacy matters to us.

This policy explains how WAFA IT SOLUTIONS handles information when you visit this website or contact us about our work.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

About this policy

This policy applies to the WAFA IT SOLUTIONS website, including its information about our company, services, products, industries, case studies, insights, careers, and legal information. It does not cover other websites or services that may be linked from this site; those providers have their own privacy practices.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email or use the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, company, and the details of your message. You may also choose to include other information relevant to an enquiry or career conversation. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

The careers page directs interested people to contact us; this website does not provide a job-application account or file-upload feature.

Information processed when you visit

Like most websites, the hosting and security infrastructure used to deliver this site may process technical request data, such as your IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, and the time of a request. This information may be used to deliver the site, protect it, diagnose errors, and maintain reliable operations.

The website code does not currently include analytics or advertising trackers, or use browser storage for those purposes. Infrastructure providers may use essential technical mechanisms to operate or secure the site.

How we use information

We use information you send to respond to your questions, discuss potential projects or career opportunities, and maintain our communications. Technical request data may be used for site delivery, security, troubleshooting, and reliability. We do not sell your personal information.

When information is shared

We may make information available to service providers that help us operate, host, or secure the website and communications, and to professional advisers where needed. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect people, our rights, or the security of our services.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry and communication information for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant business records, and meet legal obligations. Technical records are retained according to the practices of the infrastructure providers supporting the site. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of online transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You can choose what information to include when contacting us. Depending on applicable law, you may also have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

International processing and children

Our company is based in Bangladesh. Website hosting or communications providers may process information in other locations, subject to their own infrastructure and applicable safeguards. This website is intended for organizations and general professional audiences, and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The date at the top of the page indicates when it was most recently revised. Please review this page from time to time for updates.
